I am a Research Scientist in the Optimization group at [MERL](https://www.merl.com/people/deza).

I received my Ph.D. from the [Industrial Engineering & Operations Research](https://ieor.berkeley.edu/) department at UC Berkeley, where I was advised by [Alper Atamtürk](https://atamturk.ieor.berkeley.edu/). After my Ph.D., I was a Ronald J. and Carol T. Beerman Postdoctoral Fellow in the [H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering](https://www.isye.gatech.edu/) at Georgia Tech, working with [Santanu S. Dey](https://www2.isye.gatech.edu/~sdey30/). Previously, I completed my BASc in [Engineering Science](https://engsci.utoronto.ca/) at the University of Toronto, where I was advised by [Merve Bodur](https://mervebodur.github.io/).
